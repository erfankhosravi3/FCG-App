# FRIENDLY CAR GUY — DEALER SALES ACCELERATION SYSTEM
## Built for Camelback Toyota salespeople. Engineered by Erfan.

---

## THE REAL PROBLEM

Dealership salespeople at Camelback Toyota are:
1. **Slow on leads** — internet leads come in, response time is too slow, leads go cold
2. **Disorganized** — they forget who said what, miss follow-ups, lose deals in the chaos
3. **Starving for leads** — not enough fresh ups walking in or calling
4. **Leaving money on the table** — they talk to people but can't close enough deals

The dealership CRM exists but it's clunky, nobody loves it, and it doesn't actually help them sell — it just logs what already happened.

## WHAT YOU'RE BUILDING

A **sales co-pilot** that sits alongside the dealer CRM and does what it can't:
- Responds to leads in seconds (not hours)
- Tells the salesperson everything about a customer before they pick up the phone
- Auto-creates follow-up tasks so nothing falls through the cracks
- Records, transcribes, and analyzes every call — fills in the CRM for them
- Generates more leads through automated channels
- Tracks exactly how much extra money the system generates per salesperson

## THE BUSINESS MODEL

**Revenue share** — you take a cut of the extra deals they close using the system.

This means you MUST track:
- Leads generated by the system vs. organic leads
- Response time with system vs. without
- Close rate with system vs. without
- Gross profit per deal on system-sourced leads
- Total incremental revenue attributable to the system

**Pricing structure ideas:**
- 5-10% of front-end gross on system-sourced leads
- $200-500 flat fee per deal closed from a system-sourced lead
- $99-199/month per salesperson + smaller per-deal bonus

You'll know which model works after 30-60 days of real data.

### PHASE 1: SPEED-TO-LEAD ENGINE
**Goal:** Every internet lead gets a response within 60 seconds. Automatically.
**Why first:** This alone will close more deals. Speed-to-lead is the #1 factor in internet lead conversion. Studies show responding in under 5 minutes = 9x more likely to convert. Most dealerships average 45+ minutes.

#### How It Works
1. New internet lead comes in (from dealership website, TrueCar, Cars.com, AutoTrader, KBB, etc.)
2. System detects the lead (email parsing or webhook from dealer CRM)
3. Within 30 seconds, system sends personalized SMS via Twilio:

```
"Hi [Name], this is [Salesperson] at Camelback Toyota! I saw you were
looking at the [Vehicle]. Great choice — I have some info that'll help.
When's a good time to chat? I'm available now if you are."
```

4. Simultaneously sends email with:
   - Salesperson's intro
   - Vehicle photo + specs
   - "Schedule a call" link (Cal.com)
   - "Visit us" with Google Maps link to Camelback

5. Lead's response triggers immediate notification to salesperson's phone
6. All logged in Airtable — contact created, message logged, response time tracked

#### Why This Beats the Dealer CRM
- Dealer CRM sends a generic template 10-30 minutes later
- Your system sends a personalized text in 30 seconds from the salesperson's actual number
- Customer thinks the salesperson personally texted them immediately
- Customer replies to a real number, salesperson gets push notification

### PHASE 2: SMART FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM
**Goal:** No lead ever falls through the cracks. Every customer gets the right follow-up at the right time.
**Why second:** After the initial response, most salespeople lose deals because they forget to follow up or don't know what to say.

#### How It Works
1. After every customer interaction (call, text, visit), the system automatically schedules the next follow-up
2. Each morning, salesperson opens the app and sees:

```

🟢 UPCOMING (8)
   [Next 3 days of scheduled follow-ups]

📊 YOUR NUMBERS THIS WEEK
   Calls made: 47 | Connected: 23 | Appointments: 8 | Sold: 2
```

3. Tap any contact → see full history, briefing, and suggested next action
4. After each call/interaction, system auto-generates next follow-up based on what was discussed
5. If salesperson doesn't follow up by end of day, system sends them a reminder push notification

#### Follow-Up Cadence Engine (Automated Sequencing)

Different lead types get different cadences:

**Hot Lead (actively shopping, has trade-in, approved financing)**
- Day 0: Instant response (SMS + email)
- Day 0: Phone call attempt within 1 hour
- Day 1: Morning follow-up text if no show
- Day 2: Call + personalized video/text
- Day 3: "Checking in" text with inventory update
- Day 5: "Last chance" — vehicle might sell

**Warm Lead (interested, researching, 30-60 day timeline)**
- Day 0: Instant response
- Day 1: Phone call attempt
- Day 3: Educational content (buyer's guide, market update)
- Day 7: Check-in text
- Day 14: New inventory alert that matches their interest
- Day 21: "Thinking of you" + any price changes
- Day 30: Reactivation attempt

**Cold Lead (browsing, no timeline, didn't respond)**
- Day 0: Instant response
- Day 1: Phone call attempt
- Day 3: Second text
- Day 7: Email with value content
- Day 30: Monthly market update
- Day 60: "Still looking?" reactivation
- Day 90: Final attempt or archive

**Sold Customer (post-purchase)**
- Day 1: Thank you text
- Day 7: "How's the new car?" check-in
- Day 30: First service reminder
- Day 90: Referral ask
- Day 365: Anniversary + trade-up opportunity
- Every 6 months: Service reminders

### PHASE 3: CALL INTELLIGENCE
**Goal:** Every call is recorded, transcribed, and AI-analyzed. The CRM fills itself.
**Why third:** Once they're making calls (Phase 1 + 2), you capture the gold — the conversations. This is where the real data lives.

2. Call is automatically recorded (dual-channel — salesperson + customer separate)
3. After call ends, recording → Whisper → transcript

5. Airtable auto-updates with everything discussed

### PHASE 4: POWER DIALER
**Goal:** 3x the number of calls per hour. Auto-dials next number on hangup.
**Why fourth:** Once the system is tracking and transcribing, add speed. More calls = more conversations = more deals.

#### How It Works
1. Salesperson opens app → "Start Dialing" on their follow-up list
2. System calls salesperson first (their cell rings)
3. Once connected, system dials the customer
4. If customer answers → bridged into conference, recording starts
5. If no answer → logged as "No Answer", auto-dials next number
6. On hangup → quick disposition screen (Connected/Voicemail/No Answer/Callback)
7. Auto-dials next number within 5 seconds
8. Meanwhile: previous call's recording is already being transcribed + analyzed

### PHASE 5: LEAD GENERATION ENGINE
**Goal:** More leads flowing to the salespeople than they can handle.
**Why fifth:** Now the system can handle leads fast (Phase 1), follow up perfectly (Phase 2), capture intelligence (Phase 3), and dial at speed (Phase 4). Time to feed the machine.

#### Channel 1: VIN Scanner → Instant Customer Report
Salesperson walks the lot, snaps a photo or types a VIN:
1. NHTSA API decodes VIN → specs
2. Claude generates "Why this car is great for you" talking points
3. Salesperson shares personalized link with customer via text
4. Customer sees a beautiful vehicle page with the salesperson's face and contact info
5. If customer clicks "Schedule Test Drive" → auto-appointment, lead logged

**Use case:** Walk-in browses the lot after hours. Sees a car they like. QR code on the window → lands on personalized page → submits interest → salesperson gets notified instantly next morning.

#### Channel 2: "What's My Car Worth?" Trade-In Tool
Landing page promoted on social media and business cards:
1. Customer enters year/make/model/mileage/condition
2. System estimates value range (KBB API or manual ranges)
3. Captures name, email, phone
4. Auto-assigns to next salesperson in rotation
5. Instant SMS: "Hi [Name], I can get you a solid number on your [Car]. When works for a quick 5-min appraisal? - [Salesperson], Camelback Toyota"

**Why it works:** Everyone wants to know what their car is worth. It's the #1 lead magnet in automotive.

#### Channel 3: Social Media Content Pipeline
Each salesperson builds their personal brand:
1. Add VIN + photos to content queue
2. Claude generates short script: "Just got this [Year] [Vehicle] on the lot. Here's why it's a steal..."
3. Salesperson records 30-second video
4. System schedules across Instagram/TikTok/Facebook
5. All inquiries route back to salesperson's Twilio number → into the system

#### Channel 4: Automated Be-Back Campaigns
Customers who visited but didn't buy:
1. System tracks all showroom visits logged in the app
2. Day 3: "Hey [Name], that [Vehicle] you looked at is still here. Any questions I can answer?"
3. Day 7: If price dropped or incentive changed → "Good news! [Vehicle] just got a [rebate/price drop]"
4. Day 14: "Still thinking about the [Vehicle]? Happy to hold it for you — just let me know"
5. Day 30: "Hey [Name], we just got some new inventory that matches what you were looking for. Want me to send pics?"

#### Channel 5: Referral Engine
After every sold deal:
1. Day 7 post-sale: "How's the new [Vehicle]? If anyone you know is looking, I'll take great care of them — and there's a [gift card/$50/referral bonus] in it for you"
2. Track referrals in Airtable → attribute revenue to referrer
3. Auto-send thank you when referral buys

#### Channel 6: Service Drive Conquest
Customers coming in for service are the warmest leads — they already own a Toyota:
1. Service appointment data (if accessible) → identify customers with high-mileage or older vehicles
2. Auto-text before service appointment: "Hey [Name], while your [Vehicle] is in for service, want me to run a quick trade-in value? No pressure — just good info to have"
3. Track service-to-sales conversions

## LEGAL + COMPLIANCE NOTES

### Call Recording
- **Arizona is one-party consent** — salesperson is the consenting party
- **Best practice:** Add brief disclosure: "This call may be recorded for quality purposes"
- **Storage:** Twilio stores recordings, accessible via API

### SMS / TCPA
- **Internet leads:** Consent typically included in dealer website lead form (check the fine print)
- **Existing customers:** Prior business relationship allows follow-up contact
- **New outreach:** Must have express written consent for marketing texts
- **Always:** Include opt-out ("Reply STOP to unsubscribe"), honor quiet hours (8AM-9PM)
- **Use Twilio's A2P 10DLC registration** — required for business messaging

### Dealer CRM Relationship
- This system **supplements**, not replaces the dealer CRM
- Salespeople should still log deals in the official CRM (for pay plans, F&I, etc.)
- This system handles the speed, intelligence, and automation layer

### Data Privacy
- Customer data stays in your Airtable (you control it)
- Call recordings accessible only to assigned salesperson + you
- No data shared between salespeople's contact lists (unless intentional)
- PIN-protected access per user
